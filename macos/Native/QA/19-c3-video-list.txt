# Design round C3, the Video list (boards C3-02–C3-05, E3, E5, E8, E10, N1,
# N2): no selection, one checkbox per row ("process it"). New videos come in
# checked; the header's box (all / "−" / none) acts only on the rows that
# have a box; the bottom bar's button says what it does. The keyboard's ring
# (↑ ↓) and the drawers (Space → ←, a click on the row). During a batch the
# boxes become circles and do not change; a video added meanwhile comes in
# checked for the next batch. A done row checked by hand "will be redone"
# (⋯ › Elabora di nuovo does the same); the header's none puts it back.
# Retry on a failed row with no batch processes that file at once, alone (a row
# checked meanwhile stays checked); during a batch it goes «In coda, dopo il
# lotto» and starts by itself when the batch ends; Stop All drops it (its alert
# says so) and the row is failed again. The ⋯ menu has the context menu's items
# in its order. Then the boards' states, fed by hand, captured in dark and light,
# the update capsule beside the gear and the narrow window's panel (N2, no ×).
