# Design notes 77 and 78 on a video of mine (anime, not in the repository:
# SUBVISION_QA_MEDIA must hold _test-1.mkv). With the signs band on, frame
# 9 (00:00:27: "Nota: Borscht = …" at the top, "Borscht?" at the bottom)
# has text in both bands: badge for both, a sign counted. The quick probe
# no longer calls the busy drawing at 00:00:18 text (17 of 20 with text,
# none of them false), and Try OCR corrects a frame the search marked
# wrong: frame-bands plays the false positive, the test takes it away.
@needs _test-1.mkv
! cp "$MEDIA/_test-1.mkv" "$WORK/"
@launch
wait-ready 600
? ^ready
lang it
params default
inspector-set langHint it-IT
band-set top-band 1
? top on
add $WORK/_test-1.mkv
preview-open 0
? ^ok · _test-1\.mkv · searching$
wait-frames 600
? ^20 frames · 17 with text · 1 with signs
frames
? (^| )9:bottom\+top( |$)
frame 9
preview-state
? bands bottom\+top
preview-capture $OUT/frame-9-both-bands.png
? frame-9-both-bands.png$
try-ocr
? ^ok · reading
wait-ocr 120
? top ".*Borscht
preview-state
? bands bottom\+top
frame silent
? ^ok · frame
frame-bands bottom
? bands bottom
try-ocr
? ^ok · reading
wait-ocr 120
preview-state
? bands none
preview-capture $OUT/silent-after-test.png
? silent-after-test.png$
params default
